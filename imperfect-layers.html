Beauty of Clumsiness

This project began with the layers of concrete patched over cracks in the road. When a passerby notices those clumsy, stacked repairs, they can sense the warmth inside them. Our imperfection, rather than hiding it, is how we come to recognize each other's warmth.

This website grows a new layer with every visit. I can see more, and more clearly, because others came before me. And my visit makes the way a little easier for whoever comes next.
